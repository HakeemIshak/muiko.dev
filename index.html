muiko.dev

a small home on the internet.

muiko is a personal home page you furnish yourself: a profile, some notes, your favourites, and little rooms you add on as you need them. one person is building it, slowly, after work.

what a muiko home is

think of it like a flat, not a feed.

  • a door

    your profile: a name, a line about you, where to find you.

  • a notebook

    notes: short posts, newest on top, no algorithm.

  • a shelf

    favourites: links, books, places, songs worth keeping.

then you add rooms. each room is a small tool at its own address that shows up on your home page. you keep the ones you use and ignore the rest.
